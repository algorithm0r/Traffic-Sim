Traffic Sim.

an agent-based freeway: every car is a driver with its own speed, gaps, reaction time and attention
What am I looking at

  • One freeway, folded into rows like lines of text. Traffic drives left to right, leaves the end of a row and carries on at the start of the next; on the loop the last row feeds the first.
  • Ramps hang below the road on the right: drivers work across to their EXIT, and new drivers merge in from each ON ramp.
  • Each driver follows the car ahead (IDM) and changes lanes when it pays (MOBIL, LMRS), with reaction times, misjudged gaps and glances away. Four driver types, fitted to real trajectories (NGSIM I-80).
  • Car body: the same drivers in two bodies. 2D cars steer across the lane line; 1D cars jump between lane slots. Crashes are never scripted: in the 2D body they emerge from late reactions, misjudged gaps and glances away.
